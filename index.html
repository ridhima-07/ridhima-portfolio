ClauseCheck

An AI-assisted agreement-review application for text-based PDFs. It identifies important clauses, provides explanations and attention levels, and checks quoted evidence against the original document. Verified or recovered evidence can be highlighted in a downloadable PDF; unconfirmed findings are kept separate for manual review.

In development — backend completed, frontend underway
